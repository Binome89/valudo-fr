Cosmetic brands

Materials that serve your formulation and your brand promise.

The challenge

In cosmetics, a material is not chosen on its specifications alone. Its origin, properties, traceability and the quality of its documentation also contribute to the value of the finished product and the coherence of the brand.

Our answer
  • Materials selected for their properties and uses, according to your formulation needs.
  • A clear, documented origin, from the value chain to the delivered batch.
  • Controlled traceability, backed by our knowledge of value chains and their processing partners.
  • Available certifications depending on products and value chains: Organic, Fair for Life, Vegan or other applicable standards.
  • Suitable documentation: specifications, traceability elements and technical information useful for product development and value creation.

A material chosen for its origin, its properties and what it can bring to your product.
