Why choose Valúdo

A field partner,
not just a broker.

We are present at origin, alongside producers and processing partners, to control the material all the way to the delivered product.

  • Field presence, as close as possible to producers and partners.
  • Audited value chains and materials: quality, processing, traceability and social and environmental practices.
  • Controlled traceability, on our directly operated value chains as well as those developed with our partners.
  • From raw material to finished product: processing, quality control, packaging and logistics.
