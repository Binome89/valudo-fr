Our commitments

Building value chains that create value,
at origin as much as on the market.

Our ambition: to develop organic, traceable value chains, structured over time, able to create value for producers, territories and our clients.

Our mission

Creating value at every stage,
from origin to finished product.

We develop organic, traceable value chains, structured over time. Our role is to connect producers, collectors, processing partners and markets to build a value chain that is clearer, more reliable and better shared. This requirement applies as much to product quality as to the way the value chain is built.

Organic is today the foundation of our model. When it makes sense for the value chain and the market, we go further with fair-trade, local development and ecosystem-preservation approaches.

Our commitments

Five commitments, five proofs.

At Valúdo, a commitment is not a statement: it is a practice, documented and verifiable.

1 — Product excellence

Quality begins at origin and is built at every stage: material selection, process control, and batch consistency and control.

In concrete termsGold Medal, AVPA 2020 International Oils Competition, for our virgin coconut oil from São Tomé and Príncipe.

2 — Organic & fair trade

Organic is today the foundation of our value chains. When relevant for the origin, the material and market expectations, we also develop fair-trade approaches, to go further in the relationship with producers and in value sharing.

In concrete termsOur historic value chain in São Tomé and Príncipe is certified Organic and Fair for Life, with nearly ten years of hindsight. The Fair for Life standard relies notably on minimum-price mechanisms, lasting trade relationships and a development fund for local communities.

3 — Quality & analytical control

Each material has its own characteristics, its own uses and its own requirements. We adapt our control plans to products, origins and client expectations, with the support of partner laboratories in Europe.

In concrete termsPhysico-chemical, microbiological, contaminant and residue analyses depending on references and markets.

4 — Traceability

Understanding a material also means being able to explain where it comes from. We structure our value chains to document origin, production and processing partners, and to ensure suitable traceability to the delivered batch.

In concrete termsOn our most integrated value chains, notably in São Tomé and Príncipe and in Mozambique, traceability makes it possible to go back to the producer or collector.

5 — Long-term partnerships

A value chain is not built on a series of one-off purchases. We favour lasting relationships with producers, collectors and processing partners, to progressively improve quality, supply reliability and local value creation.

In concrete termsOur value chains are built and developed over time, as close as possible to local players, with monitoring specific to each origin.
Preserving ecosystems

Valuing what exists
rather than replacing it.

We don't want to pit agricultural performance against the preservation of territories. We favour models that build on existing ecosystems: wild harvesting, maintaining plantations in place, rehabilitating plots, keeping tree cover and conservation-farming practices.

The aim is to produce and add value to local resources without shifting to a logic of systematic land conversion or intensive agriculture.

No Tree Cutting, a Valúdo commitment

No Tree Cutting is not a third-party certification, but a commitment specific to Valúdo. It reflects a simple principle: preserve existing trees and ecosystems, particularly on value chains based on wild harvesting or tree-based farming systems.

Preserve before producing more.

Our certifications

Recognised standards,
explained, not just displayed.

Certifications are mobilised depending on the products, value chains and markets concerned.

EU Organic

The European organic certification governing how a product is produced, processed and marketed as organic in the EU.

Organic AB

The French organic logo, recognised on the French market, backed by EU organic regulation.

Fair for Life

A fair-trade standard relying notably on minimum-price mechanisms, lasting trade relationships and a development fund for producer communities.

Biopartenaire

A French scheme combining organic farming, lasting value-chain relationships and fair-trade commitments.

V-Label / Vegan

Certification applicable depending on references, attesting compliance with the Vegan standard. On the coconut value chains concerned, we also favour manual harvesting, without the use of animals.

USDA Organic

The organic certification recognised on the US market.

Valúdo commitment

No Tree Cutting

A commitment specific to Valúdo, in favour of keeping trees and preserving existing ecosystems.

Our teams

Excellence is also built
from within.

Building a value chain over time also means investing in the people who make it live. In São Tomé and Príncipe as in France, we encourage training, skills development, empowerment and internal growth. We seek to identify talent, help teams progress and create the conditions for everyone to take on more responsibility as Valúdo develops.

Our ambition: grow the teams at the same pace as the value chains.
