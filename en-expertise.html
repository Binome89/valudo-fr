Our expertise

The junction between
material and market.

We connect what is built at origin with what truly meets market expectations. An expertise that runs from the value chain to the finished product, with the same requirements of quality, traceability and control.

Our conviction

Many can secure a raw material. Others can read a market. Few do both.

A raw material only creates value if it serves a real product and a real market. This conviction guides our approach: understanding the origin as much as the outlet, so as never to separate the material from the product it must become.

At Valúdo, the value chain, the product and the market are built together.

Our industrial experience

Understanding a material
also means knowing how to process it.

In São Tomé and Príncipe, Valúdo directly operates its own processing facility. This industrial experience gives us a concrete understanding of yields, processes, quality constraints and the daily realities of production.

We now apply this know-how to developing new value chains, directly or with industrial partners selected in Africa, Asia and Europe.

This industrial culture lets us look at a material beyond a spec sheet or a price: we know how it is produced, processed, controlled and packaged.

Our model was born in São Tomé and Príncipe.

This is where Valúdo built its first integrated value chain, from nut collection to local processing.

Our value-chain know-how

One method,
several materials and several origins.

Our expertise is not tied to a single material. It rests on our ability to understand a value chain as a whole: its origin, its producers and collectors, its processing, its quality challenges, its logistics and its outlets.

From São Tomé to Mozambique, from West Africa to Asia, each new origin follows the same method: understand the material and its environment, select the right partners and build a reliable, traceable value chain.

Value chains that are built, not materials bought off the shelf.

Our project method

From material to market offer.

We start from your need to build the most suitable offer, then mobilise the value chain, the product and the partners required to make it happen.

01

Understand the need

Use, target, specifications, certifications, origin, volumes, format and expected positioning.

02

Identify the right value chain

Select the material, the origin and the processing partners best suited to the project.

03

Build the product

Define the grade, specifications, packaging and, when the project requires it, go all the way to a finished product, private or white label.

04

Secure the go-to-market

Quality, compliance, documentation, availability and logistics: bring together the conditions for a controlled market launch.

What sets us apart

The value chain and the market,
within the same team.

Valúdo brings together two complementary cultures: that of the field and value chains, and that of markets and product development. We can thus start from the origin to understand what a material can become, or from the market to determine what must be built at origin.

Guillaume Taufflieb

An agronomist and a field person, Guillaume has been developing agricultural value chains in Africa for over 12 years. From producers to processing, from quality to logistics, he works as close as possible to local realities to structure and steer value chains from origin to market.

With one conviction: a value chain is built first at origin.

David Toursel

Head of Markets, Marketing & Partnerships

An organic-market expert for over 15 years, David drives the construction, positioning and go-to-market of offers. From understanding client expectations to product value creation, he connects market realities to range choices and translates them into clear, relevant offers.

With one conviction: a good product only creates value if it finds its place on the market.
